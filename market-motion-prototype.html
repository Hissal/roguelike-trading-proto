The question: without any cards, does a market where each company's real value drifts with its health, and whose price wobbles randomly around a target, read well and feel alive? Flip the toggle to compare the two targets. A: the price follows real value directly, and news only matters when it's genuine. B: the price follows perceived value, which news and momentum push around and quarterly reports pull back to the truth. Which is easier to read, and more fun to trade by eye? One tick is one trading day; a month is 20 days.
