# Presentation round two: creator feedback

Source: direct creator playtest feedback, September 26, 2026. Original artifact
at `51d351b`, preserved unchanged in `presentation-round-two-prototype.html`.

A looked too plain. B and C were more interesting, and C's aesthetic was the
favorite. C was missing the earlier card icons. Selecting a card first and then
its target felt better; target-first selection felt awkward.

Whole-page scrolling was disorienting, interrupted the creator's train of
thought, and made C effectively unplayable despite its preferred appearance.
The creator requested that the important gameplay fit into one screen, with
secondary information in popup menus or scrollable side panels. All next scenes
should use card-first targeting and aesthetics related to B and C.

## Follow-up scope

Round three explores three fixed-screen compositions: a bottom hand, a right-hand
column, and a top hand. All use dark game-board styling and card icons. C is now
the initial presentation because its aesthetic and selection order were favored.
The engine remains unchanged. Further rival work remains outside scope.

The critical next human check is whether normal play stays in view at the
creator's viewport and whether drawers preserve focus and context. Source and
in-memory verification do not establish this visual outcome.
