# Presentation round three: creator feedback

Source: direct creator feedback and four attached screenshots, September 27,
2026. Original artifact at `9731403`, preserved byte-for-byte in
`presentation-round-three-prototype.html` at the creator's explicit request.

The fixed-screen experience was much nicer, required no scrolling on the
creator's setup, and presented good information. However, default sizing looked
far too zoomed out with large unused areas. The creator's screenshots show a
2555x1439 capture at 100% and a much more readable composition at 170%; another
monitor was comfortable around 140%. This is human visual evidence for round
three, not an agent browser review or validation of future revisions.

Exposure was too easy to miss: the creator still held shares in an unfavorable
trade after thinking they had exited. They liked the explicit exposure bar from
round-one B. Cards belong at the bottom; End Turn should be nearby at bottom
right to reduce pointer travel. The green theme suits standard trading, while
purple could fit a future magical interpretation. Active-power chips and report
countdowns were appreciated.

The without-this-tick's-cards quote and card-effect delta are useful but feel
cluttered as repeated text/number rows. The creator requested three arrangements
testing the company card itself, all with the same green theme, bottom hand and
bottom-right End Turn. Explore the balance of text, color and shapes, and improve
default screen use without browser zoom.

## Authorized round four

Three company-card designs share one fixed board: exposure ledger, chart-led
price story, and position instruments. Restore exposure bars and add an explicit
Sell All shortcut using the existing sale operation. Keep signed exact values
alongside visual encodings, card-first targeting, powers, report timing, and
the unchanged market engine. Use viewport-relative typography and controls.
No new market/rival rules or presentation winner are selected.
