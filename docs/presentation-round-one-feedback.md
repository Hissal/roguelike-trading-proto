# Presentation round one: creator feedback

Source: direct creator playtest feedback, September 26, 2026. Artifact captured
at `bf04c5b`; preserved as `presentation-round-one-prototype.html`.

B looked and felt best overall. Grouping most necessary information in one card
per asset made it easiest to understand, and its overall layout felt strongest.
A's colors were especially pleasant, but its separated panels required looking
around the screen to gather one company's information. B's colors were also
acceptable and possibly more thematic. C's bright palette hurt the creator's
eyes; they could not play it, found the information difficult to use, and did not
find its price graphs (which were inside a selected-company disclosure).

The creator missed the without-cards price comparison and wanted exact price
contributions permanently visible rather than behind a disclosure.

## Authorized follow-up

Build another three switchable scenes with dark colors, all necessary asset
information grouped per company, and increasing game presentation from A
(standard) through B to C (most gamified). This is another presentation pass,
not authorization to change market or card mechanics or add a rival.

Round two lives in `presentation-prototype.html`: asset dossiers, company board,
and tactics table. B's grouping is the shared starting point; no round-two winner
has been selected. Numeric and chart comparisons explicitly exclude only the
current tick's price cards, preserving the engine's same-starting-quote meaning.
