# Uncertain-market playtest — September 26, 2026

Source: creator-reported experience with `uncertain-market-prototype.html` at
`e0f5169`. This is human feedback, not agent-observed telemetry.

## Findings

The creator reported a major improvement: sharper movements and uncertain
outcomes created tension and consequential decisions. A large losing trade did
not feel arbitrary; the creator understood the risk and deliberately avoided
putting all capital into it. They spread investments more, considered actions,
and returned to news to remember each company's situation.

The unchanged cards gained useful roles: strengthening positions, protecting
exposure, and reducing potential losses. Stabilize became substantially more
useful when halving a large move mattered. Its reduction of upside still felt
awkward, but it was usable protection. No card rebalance is selected.

The creator suggested eventually mixing quieter news and lighter movement with
the sharp reactions. Preserve the sharp moves; this is a future pacing question,
not authorization to flatten volatility in the presentation pass.

Information initially felt overloaded and confusing, becoming more natural with
familiarity. The creator attributed much of the difficulty to walls of text and
lack of visual encoding. They requested previous-tick quotes for every company
without opening company details, and average entry price visible at a glance.

This supports the market direction and improved perceived agency. It does not
prove strategic balance, universal viability of diversification, or that all
cards are useful. Seed-specific outcomes and growing familiarity are confounds.
