Experiment 02 · throwaway prototype

Three cards. Two plays.

Do temporary, varied hands create worthwhile trading choices—and a card you wish you could keep?

Choose a company → trade and play up to two cards → end turn. One turn advances one market tick.

Rules & card reference

Start with $1,000 and aim for $100 profit across 12 turns. Trade whole shares freely at current quotes, paying 0.5% per trade. No borrowing or shorting. Ordinary trades do not move prices. At closing, remaining shares sell automatically with the same fee.

Draw three cards per turn, play up to two, and discard the rest. No replacement draws. The deck has two copies of each of seven powers. When it runs out, shuffle the discard pile and finish drawing. Active effects survive discarded cards. Restart repeats the same market and shuffle.

End turn applies incoming news and resolves one tick before showing the new quotes and news together. Effects then expire and the next hand arrives. Stabilize and Accelerate last one tick; pressure lasts three ticks. Only one pressure effect per company. Stabilize and Accelerate can combine, but duplicate copies cannot stack on the same company.
