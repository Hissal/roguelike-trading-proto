Quarterly reports and the news feed

The question: what do a company's quarterly report and a general news item look like, and does the split read clearly? Reports reveal what a company is really worth, and the price snaps part of the way there. News moves perceived value, sometimes for several companies at once. Mood news fades; fundamental news also changes the company and shows up in its next report. Flip News style to compare one-off items with developing stories, news told over several days whose ending is not known yet. Which gives better decisions? One more constraint: "buy good news, sell bad news" may usually work, but it must not be the best play. The strategy check at the bottom measures that. One day is one tick; a month is 20 days.

Does "buy good news, sell bad news" win?

Plays many months without you and trades by rule, at the price just after each item. Follow all news: on every ▲ buy $200 of that company, on every ▼ short $200, and close after the hold. The mood-only and fundamental-only rows do the same for one kind of news, the edge a player gets from knowing an item's kind. Follow the reports: buy on a BEAT, short on a MISS. Random is the baseline: the same trade size on a coin flip. If following the news clearly beats random in a style, that style hands out free money.
