# Prototype trends in the market

Status: open
Type: prototype
Blocked by: 04

## Question

The perceived-value market (see
[Prototype the market-motion sandbox](04-market-motion-sandbox.md)) feels a
little too random. Real stocks trend: in an uptrend the price waves with
higher highs and higher lows, the trend breaks when a new high or low fails,
and a neutral range follows before the next trend (mirrored for downtrends).
What model adds that structure while keeping the waves, and does it make the
chart more readable and tradeable by eye? Where does a trend live: in real
value (company health), in perceived value (market mood and momentum), or
both? Can a player spot a trend forming and a trend breaking without chart
expertise?
