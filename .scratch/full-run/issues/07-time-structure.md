# Prototype how a month breaks into play

Status: open
Type: prototype
Blocked by: 04

## Question

How does a month (one encounter of four weeks) break into gameplay? Compare with a toggle: A, play cards once per week then trade freely while the week runs in real time with live graphs and news; B, discrete day turns (3–5 per week) with week turns acting differently. Which keeps decisions quick and pressure low?
