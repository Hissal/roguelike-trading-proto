# Prototype how a month breaks into play

Status: open
Type: prototype
Blocked by: 04

## Question

How does a month (one encounter of four weeks) break into gameplay? Compare with a toggle: A, play cards once per week then trade freely while the week runs in real time with live graphs and news; B, discrete day turns (3–5 per week) with week turns acting differently. Which keeps decisions quick and pressure low?

Constraint from
[Prototype quarterly reports and the news feed](06-reports-and-news-format.md):
reading and analysing news is very draining, and a constant flood of it will
make players quit. News should be read rarely compared with the core loop,
for example read between rounds and traded on within them. An idea for how it
could show, not a plan.
