Operate a small engine

Can you combine information and influence into a clever trade—and choose which opportunity to leave behind?

How to play · take your time

Start with $1,000. Aim for $100 profit over 18 market steps, then see how much further you can go. Select a company, read the news, and buy whole shares. Sell to free up cash. Prices only move when you advance the market; ordinary trades never move them.

Investigate reveals underlying value temporarily. Price tends toward that value, but news pressure can delay or oppose the move. Influence adds upward pressure. Your Informed Influence modifier strengthens it when the company is currently revealed. You have only two uses of each power for the entire session. Remaining shares sell automatically at the close. No timer; allow roughly 5–10 minutes. Refreshing loses the session.
